# AI Career Readiness Assistant — For International Students in Australia

*AI-powered resume feedback and interview preparation for international students in Australia*

![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-Part_A_complete-blue)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-green)

## The problem
International students in Australia face persistent barriers to graduate employment — unfamiliarity with local recruitment practices, limited professional networks, and restricted access to personalised career guidance, as university career services struggle to meet growing demand at scale.

## The evidence
- Generative AI can deliver instant, adaptive, context-aware feedback for resumes and interview preparation
- Existing AI-based career tools are fragmented — most address a single function (resume review *or* interview practice) rather than an integrated experience
- Algorithmic bias, transparency, and privacy concerns remain significant barriers to trusted adoption of AI in career support

## The solution
An AI-powered platform that integrates resume feedback and interview preparation into one user-centred experience, purpose-built for international students navigating the Australian job market.

- Combines two core functions (resume feedback + interview preparation) rather than the single-function tools currently available
- Embeds a responsible AI governance layer aligned to the NIST AI Risk Management Framework from the ground up
- Designed to complement — not replace — existing university career services

## Ethics, privacy and responsible AI
Built on the ACS Code of Professional Ethics, the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework. Fairness, transparency, and human oversight are treated as design requirements — users are informed when they're receiving AI-generated content and are directed to verify recommendations against other sources. Full detail in [`/docs`](./docs).

## Sustainability
Built on cloud-based AI services rather than training models from scratch, minimising computational and energy costs. Designed for equitable access across the international student population and aligned with WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standards.
